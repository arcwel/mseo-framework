# What to Do About a Chipped Tooth in Sampleton

<!-- block: answer -->
**Rinse, save any piece you can find, and book a visit with the practice. Most chips are repaired in one appointment.**

<!-- block: steps -->
## What should I do in the first hour?

1. Rinse with warm water.
2. Keep the piece in milk if you found it.
3. Cover a sharp edge with sugar-free gum.

<!-- block: why -->
## Why does a small chip matter?

A chip exposes the layer under the enamel, which is why a tooth can feel sensitive to cold after a knock.

<!-- block: local -->
## Where can Sampleton families ask about mouthguards?

The Sampleton Back-to-School Health Fair at the Sampleton Library on 2026-11-14 has a dental table, and a fitted mouthguard is the simplest way to avoid the next chip.

Illustration: how a mouthguard sits over the front teeth.

<!-- block: questions -->
## Can I wait until my next checkup?

If the tooth is not sore, a week is fine. Pain or a loose piece means call the practice today. New patients welcome.

<!-- block: faq -->
## Frequently asked questions

### Does a chipped tooth need a crown?

No. A small chip is usually smoothed or bonded in one visit.

### Will the repair match my other teeth?

Bonding is colour-matched before it is set.

### Can kids chip teeth at the Health Fair?

Yes, and the [Sampleton Back-to-School Health Fair](https://sampleton.example.gov/fair) on 2026-11-14 is a good day to ask us about mouthguards.
