# Where to Get Your Car Fixed in Testville

**Short answer: any licensed shop you choose. Ask the questions below before you hand over the keys.**

<!-- block: scenario -->
## What does the first evening look like?

Picture a slow bump in a crowded lot during the Harvest Fair. Nobody is hurt and the bumper is scuffed.

<!-- block: decisions -->
## Which decisions are yours?

1. **Where the car goes.** The choice is yours.
2. **Who writes the estimate.** [Request a complimentary estimate](/estimate/).

<!-- block: checklist -->
## What should you bring to the estimate?

- Photos of the damage
- Your claim number, if you have one

The [county guide](https://example-shop.test/guide/) has more on what to bring.

<!-- block: local -->
## When does the Harvest Fair change the plan?

The Testville Harvest Fair runs on 2026-11-15 at the County Fairgrounds, and the lot fills early. Park at the far end, away from the entrance queue, and photograph any new ding before you leave.

<!-- block: faq -->
## Frequently asked questions

### Can I choose my own shop?

Yes. You can pick any licensed shop.

### Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead helps us plan the day. *Call* or use the [estimate form](/estimate/).
